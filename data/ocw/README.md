# MIT OpenCourseWare exams

Past exams from six MIT courses, used to seed the demo and as the source of the
ingestion test set. Download or refresh them with `python bench/fetch_ocw.py`;
`manifest.json` lists every file and the OCW resource it came from.

| Course | Term | Instructors | Exams | Practice |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6.006 Introduction to Algorithms | Spring 2020 | Erik Demaine, Jason Ku, Justin Solomon | 3 quizzes, final, 3 quiz reviews | 9 problem sets, 9 problem sessions |
| 6.046J Design and Analysis of Algorithms | Spring 2015 | Erik Demaine, Srini Devadas, Nancy Lynch | 2 quizzes, final | 10 problem sets |
| 18.06 Linear Algebra | Spring 2010 | Gilbert Strang | 3 exams, final | |
| 18.06SC Linear Algebra (OCW Scholar) | Fall 2011 | Gilbert Strang | 3 exams, final | 31 session problem sheets |
| 6.042J Mathematics for Computer Science | Fall 2010 | Tom Leighton, Marten van Dijk | midterm, practice midterm, finals 2004–2010 | 12 problem sets, 23 recitations |
| 6.041 Probabilistic Systems Analysis and Applied Probability | Fall 2010 | John Tsitsiklis | quizzes and finals, 2008–2010 | 11 problem sets, 11 tutorials, 24 recitations |
| 6.034 Artificial Intelligence | Fall 2010 | Patrick Winston | quizzes and finals, 2008–2010 | |

Exams are in each course folder and practice material in its `practice/`
subfolder. Solutions are not included. The 2010 and 2011 runs of 18.06 are
seeded as one course.
